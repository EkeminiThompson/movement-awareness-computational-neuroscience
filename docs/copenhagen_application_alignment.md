# Copenhagen Postdoctoral Application: Alignment Notes

## Vacancy

University of Copenhagen, Department of Psychology, Postdoc in Cognitive Computational Neuroscience and Human Movement Perception. Project: *Mapping Experience of Action: Redefining Theories of Consciousness*. Official vacancy: https://jobportal.ku.dk/alle-opslag/?show=169455

The vacancy asks applicants to request the original project proposal from Associate Professor Mark Schram Christensen (markc@psy.ku.dk) before submitting. The short project description is limited to two standard pages / approximately 4,800 characters including title, punctuation, figures, footnotes and references, excluding bibliography. The research plan is limited to two pages. Other required documents include a CV (maximum three pages), one-page cover letter, degree documentation, publication list, and up to three selected publications.

## Proposed contribution (provisional)

A defensible computational contribution is to model how pre-action neural and bodily signals relate to subsequent movement and subjective movement experience, using behavioural/psychophysical measures available in the host project's own design. The IACKD analysis may demonstrate multimodal data-handling and participant-independent modelling skills, but it does not replace the host project's planned experiments or directly measure subjective movement awareness.

Potential contributions to discuss with the host team:
- hierarchical or state-space models linking trial-wise neural/kinematic signals to subjective reports, if collected by the host project's experiments;
- participant-level and trial-level computational models that distinguish prediction, postdictive inference, and confidence;
- model comparison and out-of-sample evaluation;
- transparent preregistered analysis and reproducible pipelines;
- integration of computational predictions with psychophysical manipulations and philosophical distinctions in the main project.

## Required next step

Request and read the original project proposal before finalising the two-page project description. Do not invent the project's experimental manipulations, hypotheses, measures, or theoretical commitments. Tailor the application to the proposal once received.
